Known by many a citizen in Vornaud, The Coincounter Knights are a vigilante paladin order in Vornaud. Though none have ever been caught to confirm their doctrine, it is believed they are a paladin order for Virellus, believing that wealth should be equitably distributed, attacking corrupt businesses to steal their coin and giving it to the poor and needy in the city. As such, The Coincounter Knights are particularly popular among the lower-class in Vornaud, despite being labelled as dangerous criminals by the Vornaud Council.

#### Members
The Coincounter Knights number 7, And all of the members wear somewhat mismatched gear. most of it looks well used and well-maintained, with plenty of scuff marks and scratches to denote them being used.

##### Commander Morgrun Firemane
Race: Dwarf
Gender: Male
Age: "Middle-Aged" for a Dwarf, approximately 200 years old
Appearance: abnormally dilated brown eyes, long brown plaited hair, and a similarly braided beard that has blackened marks across it, like someone took a blowtorch to random bits of their beard. what you can see from his face is not scarred, but definitely "worn", with heavy forehead lines and a large nose.

Commander of the Coincounter Knights. incredibly dedicated and also probably insane, he created the Coincounter Knights and recruits people crushed by the corrupt upper echelons of Vornaud to fight back. this makes his people loyal to him, even if his beliefs could be considered 'extreme'

##### Cyrano
