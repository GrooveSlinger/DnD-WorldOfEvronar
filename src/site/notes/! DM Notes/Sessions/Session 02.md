Ran: Feb 7th



#### Recap/Intro

*Last we left off, we found our Hero- well, maybe a bit too presumptuous to be calling them heroes just yet. Our Adventurers, we'll say, in a tavern. some meeting for the first time, all to take a Job by one Rakin informant named DeeDee. The Job sounded simple on parchment, retrieve a Pre-Fracture Artifact in some ruins a few days outside of Vornaud, but some prodding from Gilbert determined that whatever this job is, it must be pretty important for whoever wants it.*

*They began their journey, with 2 of their member immediately transforming into modes of transportation for the rest of the group. Unfortunately for them, on the 2nd day of their journey they were waylaid by some Bandits, those of the Blue Devils*

*After a particularly gruesome battle, resulting in the awakening of some kind of magic briefcase and some... recontextualising of what the Adventuring life is like for Norman, the group continued on and finally made it to Echo Hall, the ruins they were directed to. noticing that it looked like there was some recent activity here, perhaps from other Adventurers, they made their way inside.*

*From here they were able to quickly dispatch remnants of the defenses of Echo Hall, and deduced the small riddle that the Ekovon Order used to guard some kind of vault, they made their way deeper into Echo Hall and found the last of the previous adventuring party meet their end to the defenses, before shortly engaging our party.*

*After a fierce fight with the animated armors that defended the vault room, Our party was victorious and the scattered and scorched remains littered the room. and here, with their obstacles removed between them and the artifact, is where we pick up our story. So, What would you like to do?*
