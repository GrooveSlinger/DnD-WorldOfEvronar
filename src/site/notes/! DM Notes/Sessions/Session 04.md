Ran: March 15th



#### Recap/Intro

*Last we left off, Our Adventurers found themselves in Gilberts workshop, after uncovering some information that seems to link Norman, Gilbert, and Daisy together. They discussed and agreed that Norman should keep a low profile, and decided to start shopping around town using their newfound wealth from the previous job. after stopping in at a general supplies store, they learned that while our adventurers were out, it was announced that a ceremony would be occurring in Traders Square for the 150th Anniversary of the Virellus Reserve on Sunday.*

*They continued shopping and upon entering the Blacksmiths shop of "Slash n' Smash", learned from the shopkeep that his blacksmith brother has been missing for the past 3 days. After some negotiating with a man who probably shouldn't be in charge of financial decisions, they agreed that if they can find and return his brother, then they can receive a magical greataxe that caught the eye of Lagaruk.*

*Making their way to the docks, the last area the brother was spotted, they decided to ask around in the nearby tavern The Salty Siren. inside, they asked the Barkeep who was decidedly unhelpful in their endeavour, but they noticed a group in the corner acting suspicious when the name "Gurgash" was mentioned. As the group went over to talk, they attacked.*

*After a big brawl resulting in a good bit of property damage, a lot of slipping in grease, and a leg being accidentally torn off, they interrogated a tiefling member of the group by the name of "Chant". he revealed that Gurgash was being held in Warehouse 4 Bay 6, and the reason he was grabbed was because he saw a bribe taking place between a Dockmaster and a businessman. upon being pressed, Chant gave details about the business man that seemed to Describe Norman B, who was seemingly kidnapped just yesterday.*

*The group made their way to the warehouse. Lagaruk had a... anti-climactic meeting with Gurgash, and Gilbert investigated the rest of the warehouse, which appeared to be filled with barrels of "Firebrand Wine". However, on closer inspection, seemed to be loaded to the brim with Gunpowder.*

*and that... is where we left off. what would you like to do?*


#### Summary
Freed Gurgash. after some deliberating they decided to leave the gunpowder where it is and make it look undisturbed.

Escorted Gurgash back to his shop and got the axe, to which Gurgash was a bit grumpy by. they then returned to Gilberts workshop, installed Daisy's stake thrower, and had a long rest

Norman woke up early and made some muesli bar treats, aswell as baked beans and eggs for everyone for breakfast. they then went over to The Lucky Dip, and Grub cashed in her favor for them to learn everything they can about the Coincounter Knights.

Went back to traders square, Lagaruk stopped in on Slash n' Smash and had an awkward conversation with Agronak, and then left before Gurgash realised (and was seemingly disappointed) that Lagaruk didn't speak with him.

They found a discrete sewer entrance and went in and started exploring, they briefly explored the sewer where they believed they saw a Karate Rat, but did not continue. they then found a small pit of acid and made their way across (rope swing, Norman rolled a nat 20 and did a perfect backflip ) and found themselves at another crossroads.
