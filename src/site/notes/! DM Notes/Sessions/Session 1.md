#### Summary

DeeDee gave them the job to locate a pre-fracture artifact in Echo Hall, 3 days to the Southwest. Gilbert deduced with a nat 20 insight that this is clearly a job that is a big deal in the information community.

they went on their way and interacted with each other during the journey. Along the way, they were approached by a small bandit group trying to rob them. unfortunately for them, they didn't know that the cart was actually a warforged and compressed one of them. Norman also experienced his first bit of magic with the Briefcase. he also experienced a small wild magic surge (rolled 77. next corpse he touches he casts speak with dead on it). Gilbert got special goggles that allow him to see, and Lagaruk found a "Cool Tankard" (Gilbert Identified it as a Tankard of Sobriety, but decided not to tell Lagaruk). 

They continued their journey into Echo Hall. after dealing with some Gray Ooze that seeped out of the armors. they found a small prayer room containing the Ekovon Oath, which allowed them to proceed deeper into Echo Hall, however, not before Gilbert had entered the nearby armory and disabled some animated armor and found some extra treasure.

They proceeded deeper into the Hall, avoiding a trap by remembering the etched writing and keeping the Oath close to them, before entering a large room containing the artifact, aswell as the guardian of the artifact and the dispatched members of a previous adventuring party who attempted to get it. After a long battle, through the clever work of the whole party, especially Grub's Flaming Sphere and the Unbridled Fury of Lagaruk, they dispatched the guardians of this artifact and are poised to retrieve that which they were hired to do.
