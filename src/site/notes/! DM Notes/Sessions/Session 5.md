Ran: April 18th




#### Recap/Intro

*Last we left off, our Adventurers found themselves in Warehouse 4, Bay 6, with the chained up Gurgash Gro'Malog and barrels of gunpowder. after some deliberation, they decided to leave the gunpowder as is to prevent any suspicion and escorted Gurgash back to his shop. as reward, they were given the axe that caught Lagaruk's eye, though Gurgash didn't seem very pleased about it*

*They made their way back to Gilbert's workshop, and after a long rest and some breakfast prepared by Norman, began investigating further in regards to the Coincounter Knights and the kidnapped Norman B. as part of this, they made their way back to The Lucky Dip, and Grub cashed in their favor to get information from DeeDee about them*

*They learned that the Coincounter Knights were spotted carrying an unconscious body with them the last time they made their rounds in The Commons, and not only that, that it's believed they might be in the sewers near the Virellus Reserve, and that for some reason, they still entered the commons to pick up trash despite having someone with them*

*The Adventurers made their way to Trader's Square, and located a suitable entrance to the sewers away from prying eyes, albeit some distance from the Virellus Reserve. encountering some large carrion crawlers through the sewers, and what appeared to be a humanoid rat doing Karate, they ventured through the sewers and past a pipe of leaking acid before reaching a part they believe to be under the main square.*

*And that, is where we last left off. but before the story continues, I need all of you to make wisdom saving throws.*



#### Summary

Fought the kungfu rats and learned that they were from Haxi (from Kitai, likely), shortrested near a cistern and looted their dojo. gave trash to the otyugh, and managed to persuade The Coincounter Knights to let them have a more reasonable discussion.
