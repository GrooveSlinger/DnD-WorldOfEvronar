Haxi is a continent located to the southwest of Turogarde. it is equatorial, making it quite humid and jungle-like in its environs.

During the [[History/War of Fracture\|War of Fracture]], it is believed that Aurendil cast a spell so powerful that it carved a divide between this continent, turning the once mostly circular continent into a twin of teardrop shaped islands.

Post-Fracture, these two islands developed independently of each other and developed differing cultures, and as of the past 300 years have been quite antagonistic towards each other
